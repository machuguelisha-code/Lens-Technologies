Method

A research workflow that survives review

  • Frame the question so it can be answered with sources you can obtain.
  • Map the law: constitution, statute, subsidiary legislation, case law, then commentary.
  • Verify every provision against the current consolidation and amendments.
  • Record citations as you read; never reconstruct them later.
  • Separate what the source says from what you argue it means.
  • Disclose data, coding rules and limitations in an appendix.
