Free

Monthly clinics in rural wards with paralegals, focused on land, succession and gender-based violence.

Paid

Consultancy

Corporate advisory, compliance audits, contract drafting, dispute strategy and training, billed hourly or per project.

What to expect

Your first consultation, step by step

  • Intake (10 min) — we record the facts and check for conflicts of interest.
  • Assessment (15 min) — the advocate identifies the legal issue and the forum.
  • Options (5 min) — each route with its cost, timeline and risk.
  • Written summary — emailed or printed the same day.
  • Means test — if you qualify, the matter moves to the pro bono docket.

Everything you say is confidential. We will never charge for the first 30 minutes.
