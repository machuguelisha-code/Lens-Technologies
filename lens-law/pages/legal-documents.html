How to use a template

A template is a starting point, never the answer

  • Read the drafting note first: it tells you which clauses are mandatory.
  • Replace every bracketed placeholder; unfilled placeholders defeat a document.
  • Check execution: attestation, witnesses, commissioner for oaths, company seal.
  • Confirm stamp duty and registration deadlines before signature.
  • When value or risk is high, have an advocate review the final draft.
