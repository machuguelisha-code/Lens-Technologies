Appearing before the Bench

Etiquette that earns you a hearing

  • Be robed and seated before the court rises; announce appearances clearly.
  • Address the court correctly: "My Lord/My Lady" in the High Court and above.
  • File and serve within time; ask for indulgence only with an affidavit.
  • Answer the question the judge asked before returning to your submission.
  • Never misstate an authority: correct the record the moment you notice.
Practice notes

What the Bench keeps repeating

Plead jurisdiction

State the forum's pecuniary and territorial jurisdiction on the face of the pleading.

Respect time limits

Delay must be accounted for day by day; inadvertence is rarely sufficient cause.

Cite accurately

Neutral citation, court, year and the exact paragraph relied on.

Serve properly

Defective service is the most common cause of wasted hearing dates.

Use assessors correctly

In tribunals, record their opinions; omission is a ground of appeal.

Settle where possible

Mediation is mandatory in several tracks and is measured in the case audit.
