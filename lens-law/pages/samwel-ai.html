Samwel AI

Your first stop for a legal question. Samwel explains the position in plain language, names the statute or authority to read, and tells you when a human advocate is the only safe answer.

Ask a question

Talk to Samwel

Samwel answers only on Tanzanian law, from a curated corpus of the Laws of Tanzania and the decided cases of the Court of Appeal indexed on TanzLII. It runs in your browser: your question is never sent anywhere.

Samwel gives legal information, not legal advice. For a decision on your own facts, book a consultation.

What Samwel is good at

  • Explaining a procedure step by step
  • Pointing to the governing Act and section
  • Listing the documents you must gather
  • Flagging limitation periods and deadlines
  • Answering in English or Kiswahili
  • Finding Court of Appeal decisions on the point, with citations

What it will never do

  • Predict how a named judge will rule
  • Draft a final court document unreviewed
  • Store or share your personal facts
How it works

Retrieval first, generation second

1. Retrieve

Your question is matched against the Lens Law corpus: statutes, TanzLII judgments, practice directions and our own digests.

2. Ground

Only retrieved passages are used to compose the answer, so every statement can be traced to a source.

3. Hand over

Where the question needs judgement or facts, Samwel routes you to the legal aid desk instead of guessing.
