Method

How a matter becomes a data point

  • Sampling frame: judgments published on TanzLII plus our own case files.
  • Double coding: two coders per matter, disagreements resolved by a third.
  • Variables: forum, area, date filed, date decided, stages, outcome, appeal.
  • No personal data: parties are pseudonymised before analysis.
  • We publish duration and outcome distributions, never judge-level predictions.
