Method

How a judgment becomes a data point

  • Sampling frame: the Court of Appeal collections published on TanzLII, retrieved 19 August 2026.
  • Volumes per year and per registry are TanzLII's own counts, not our estimates.
  • Area of law is coded from TanzLII's subject headnotes; outcome from its case summary.
  • Where a summary does not state the result we record "Not stated in summary" rather than guess.
  • Every case carries its neutral citation and a link, so you can verify before you cite.
  • No judge-level outcome predictions: the coram of each judgment is not in the dataset.

These are published court records, not official Judiciary statistics. Read the full judgment before relying on any entry.
