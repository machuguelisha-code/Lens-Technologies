Search technique

Five habits of a good free-law search

  • Search the section number with the Act name, not the topic alone.
  • Use quotation marks for a phrase of art, e.g. "unfair termination".
  • Filter by court: a High Court decision does not bind the Court of Appeal.
  • Check the date of a consolidated Act against later amendments.
  • Record the neutral citation the moment you find a judgment.
