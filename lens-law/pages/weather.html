Why a law site carries weather

Rain moves hearings and closes roads

During the long rains, ward tribunals in low-lying districts are frequently inaccessible, witnesses cannot travel, and mobile clinics must be re-routed. Weather is an operational input for access to justice, not a curiosity.

  • Plan outreach clinics around heavy-rainfall days
  • Warn clients travelling to distant court stations
  • Evidence for adjournment applications caused by weather
  • Context for land, insurance and agricultural disputes
